# The Altar Initiative — brand direction

## Brand foundation

The product belongs to an initiative that invites the church into a shared daily rhythm of prayer: morning, noon, and evening. Its public voice should point people toward Jesus, Scripture, worship, and prayer for awakening in the region.

Use language of invitation, shared rhythm, worship, consecration, and participation. Avoid language that makes volunteers sound interchangeable or implies that prayer is a productivity metric.

## Naming system

- **The Altar Initiative** — initiative and product family name.
- **Morning Altar** — public morning worship-led gathering.
- **Noon prayer moment** — personal or shared pause wherever someone is; not necessarily a room booking.
- **Evening Altar** — public evening worship-led gathering.
- **Prayer focus** — a Scripture-centered daily focus.
- **Serve interest** — the public invitation to explore serving; never “claim a shift.”

## Public-page message hierarchy

1. **The Altar Initiative**
2. **A daily prayer rhythm**
3. Morning • Noon • Evening
4. A concise invitation to join in worship, Scripture, and prayer for awakening.
5. Calls to action: **View gatherings**, **Receive updates**, **Explore serving**.

Keep internal coordination language out of the public page: no “coverage,” “unfilled,” “schedule gap,” or volunteer roster.

## Visual direction

The source announcement uses a restrained, contemplative treatment: cool muted blue-green, soft grain, warm off-white type, generous spacing, uppercase letterspaced display type, and a refined italic accent. Carry the mood forward rather than reproducing a document layout.

Suggested product tokens:

```text
Deep altar teal     #3F5F5B
Muted sage          #6F8580
Warm parchment      #F5F1E8
Soft stone          #D9D3C6
Ink                 #1F2421
Accent gold         #B99A61
```

Use teal, parchment, and gold sparingly for atmosphere and hierarchy. Private coordinator views should prioritize clarity and accessibility over the public site’s more devotional expression.

## Example microcopy

- “Join the daily rhythm of prayer.”
- “Morning, noon, and evening—turning our attention to Jesus together.”
- “Explore serving with the Altar Initiative.”
- “Receive gathering updates and daily prayer focuses.”
- “Your service helps make space for shared worship and prayer.”
