# Altar Initiative — decision log

## 2026-08-31 — Public and volunteer experiences are separate

The public site will show visitor-safe gatherings and an invitation to serve. It will not expose open volunteer shifts, volunteer identities, operational gaps, or internal notes.

## 2026-08-31 — Volunteer access is approval-based

Visitors may express interest but cannot self-register for shifts. Coordinator approval precedes an invitation-only volunteer account.

## 2026-08-31 — No prayer-request processing

The product will not intake, store, route, or report on prayer requests.

## 2026-08-31 — Messaging is consent-based

Email and SMS preferences are separate. Scheduling SMS is opt-in only, and every send is auditable.

## 2026-08-31 — Altar Initiative language and rhythm guide the product

Use “The Altar Initiative” as the product name and a “daily prayer rhythm” as its organizing metaphor. Public-facing product language may name the Morning Altar, noon prayer moment, Evening Altar, shared Scripture/Psalms and worship resources, and prayer for awakening in our region. These concepts support the product but do not change its access boundaries.
